What's Inside

Every module a luxury storefront actually needs.

Not a generic storefront with a coat of paint — MAISON is built around the specific mechanics of high-consideration, high-value purchases.

360° Studio & Web-AR

Let a shopper examine a piece the way they would in a boutique — spin it, zoom into the finish, then place it in their own room without installing an app.

  • Smooth, momentum-based 360° turntable viewer
  • Native Web-AR via USDZ (iOS) and GLB (Android)
  • Zero third-party app required — works in the browser
360° / AR viewer screenshot
/images/product-360.jpg

Digital Provenance Passport

Every piece can carry a cryptographic SHA-256 certificate — sourcing map, materials, and craftsmanship history a buyer can verify, not just read.

  • Tamper-evident authenticity record per product
  • Sourcing map with origin and material detail
  • Builds trust for high-value, considered purchases
Digital passport screenshot
/images/passport.jpg

Made-to-Measure & Monogramming

Bundle builder, size-chart-aware variant matrices, and live monogram preview — personalization that reflects in the price and the order, not just the UI.

  • SKU-level variant and bundle configuration
  • Real-time monogram / initials preview
  • Personalization options priced server-side
Personalization / monogram UI
/images/monogram.jpg

Tamper-Proof Checkout

Pricing, coupons, tax, and wallet deductions are recalculated server-side at checkout — a shopper's browser can never dictate what they pay.

  • Server-authoritative pricing on every order
  • Stripe, PayPal, bank wire, and cardless EMI
  • Asynchronous webhook settlement for card orders
Checkout flow screenshot
/images/checkout.jpg

A Complete Admin Suite

Catalog, orders, coupons, outlets, blog, size charts, and store settings — fully responsive, so an order can be dispatched from a phone.

  • 25+ real-time management modules
  • Role-based access with route-level protection
  • Rate-limited, fail-closed admin middleware
Admin dashboard screenshot
/images/admin-dashboard.jpg

Native-Feel PWA, Global-Ready

Installable on a home screen with offline caching, plus true Arabic RTL mirroring and localized currencies for expansion into new markets.

  • Service-worker-backed offline support
  • English, Bengali, and Arabic (RTL) included
  • Auto-formatted currencies: USD, EUR, SAR, AED, KWD
